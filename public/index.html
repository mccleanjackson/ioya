How it works

Three steps from receipt to repaid.

No more mental math at the table or awkward "you still owe me" texts a week later.

  1. Scan the receipt

    Take a photo or upload one. IOYA reads the items and prices for you, and you can fix anything it misses.

  2. Match the items

    Pick your friends or a saved group, then tap who had each item. Shared items split evenly.

  3. Send the requests

    IOYA adds tax, tip and fees in proportion and opens each person's request in their payment app.

  • Built for real receiptsLong product codes become readable names, and you can always edit an item or add one by hand.
  • Your contacts, savedSave friends with their Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, Cash App or Apple Cash details, and group the people you eat with most.
  • Fair, to the centTax, tip and fees are shared by what each person ordered, and the totals always add up to the bill.
  • Private by defaultEach profile sees only its own contacts and splits. Passwords are stored securely by Supabase, never in IOYA's tables.
